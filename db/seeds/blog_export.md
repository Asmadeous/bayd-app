## 4. Gel-X explained
**URL:** https://www.beautyservicesatyourdoor.com/post/gel-x-explained  
**Date:** Sep 4, 2023  
**Read time:** 2 min  
**Author:** Beauy at your door

Gel-X nails have gained popularity because they offer a balance between durability and a natural aesthetic. They are a great option for those who want to enhance their nails without the bulkiness often associated with traditional acrylics. However, it's important to have Gel-X nails applied and removed by a skilled nail technician to ensure the best results and minimize the risk of damage to your natural nails.

**Facts:**

Gel-X nails are a type of nail enhancement technique that involves the application of soft gel nail tips to your natural nails. Here's a more detailed explanation of Gel-X nails:

1. **Soft Gel Material:** Gel-X nails are made using soft gel materials. These gel tips are softer, more flexible, and lightweight compared to traditional acrylic nail extensions.

2. **Pre-Made Tips:** Gel-X nails come in pre-made nail tip forms that are designed to fit various nail shapes and sizes. They are available in different lengths and styles, allowing for customization to suit your preferences.

3. **Application Process:** The process of applying Gel-X nails typically involves the following steps:
   - Preparation: Your natural nails are cleaned, shaped, and lightly buffed to create an ideal surface.
   - Tip Application: A skilled nail technician selects the appropriate Gel-X tips and attaches them to your natural nails using a specially formulated adhesive gel.
   - Shaping: The technician shapes and files the Gel-X tips to achieve the desired length and shape.
   - Gel Polish: After the tips are in place, they can be coated with gel polish, allowing for various nail art and color options.
   - Curing: The nails are cured under UV or LED light to set the gel polish and adhesive properly.

4. **Natural Appearance:** One of the key advantages of Gel-X nails is their ability to provide a more natural look and feel compared to some other nail extension methods. They are thinner and more flexible, mimicking the appearance of natural nails.

5. **Durability:** Gel-X nails are known for their durability. When applied correctly and maintained well, they can last for several weeks without chipping or lifting.

6. **Removal:** Gel-X nails can be removed by soaking them in acetone or by seeking professional help. They are generally easier to remove than acrylic nails.

**What makes Gel-X a safe nail enhancement system?**

Gel-X nails are generally considered a safer option compared to traditional acrylic nails for several reasons:

1. **Less Harsh Chemicals:** Gel-X nails use soft gel materials that are less harsh on the natural nails and emit fewer strong odors than acrylics. The application process typically involves less exposure to potentially harmful chemicals.

2. **Lightweight and Flexible:** Gel-X nails are known for their flexibility and lightweight feel. This can reduce the risk of excessive pressure on the natural nails, which can lead to damage or breakage.

3. **Less Damage to Natural Nails:** When applied and removed properly by a trained technician, Gel-X nails are less likely to cause significant damage to the natural nails. This is in contrast to acrylic nails, which can sometimes weaken and thin the natural nails.

4. **Natural Appearance:** Gel-X nails provide a more natural look and feel compared to some bulkier acrylic nail extensions, which can be more aesthetically pleasing to many individuals.

## 8. The importance of Nail Care for the Elderly
**URL:** https://www.beautyservicesatyourdoor.com/post/the-importance-of-nail-care-for-the-elderly  
**Date:** Jul 7, 2023  
**Read time:** 2 min  
**Author:** Beauy at your door

**Introduction:**  
As we age, certain aspects of our self-care routine become more challenging. One area that often gets overlooked is nail care. However, maintaining healthy nails is crucial for the elderly population's overall well-being. In this blog post, we will explore the importance of nail care for the elderly and provide some practical tips for maintaining healthy nails.

1. Preventing Infections:  
As we age, our nails may become brittle, thickened, or more prone to splitting. Neglected nails can create an entry point for bacteria and fungi, leading to infections such as paronychia or fungal nail infections. Proper nail care, including regular trimming and cleaning, can significantly reduce the risk of such infections.

2. Avoiding Ingrown Nails:  
Ingrown nails occur when the nail grows into the surrounding skin, causing pain, inflammation, and even infection. For elderly individuals with mobility issues or vision impairments, proper nail trimming becomes even more crucial to prevent ingrown nails. Regularly trimming nails straight across and avoiding cutting them too short can help mitigate this problem.

3. Maintaining Overall Hygiene:  
Nail care is an essential aspect of personal hygiene. Dirty or unkempt nails can harbor dirt, bacteria, and other pathogens, increasing the risk of illness. Encouraging regular handwashing and cleaning under the nails can help prevent the spread of germs and maintain overall cleanliness.

4. Reducing Discomfort and Pain:  
Long nails can cause discomfort and pain, especially for older adults who may have sensitive or arthritic fingers. Keeping nails neatly trimmed can alleviate this discomfort, enhance manual dexterity, and improve overall hand function.

5. Enhancing Self-esteem:  
Well-groomed nails contribute to a person's overall appearance and can boost self-esteem. Engaging in nail care routines and maintaining neat and clean nails can help elderly individuals feel more confident and maintain a positive self-image.

Nail care is an often overlooked aspect of personal hygiene, particularly among the elderly. However, maintaining healthy nails is vital for preventing infections, discomfort, and maintaining overall well-being. By implementing simple nail care routines and seeking assistance when needed, older adults can enjoy healthier, more comfortable hands and nails.

## 12. Getting your nails done shouldn’t make you sick: The dangers of dipping powder and MMA
**URL:** https://www.beautyservicesatyourdoor.com/post/getting-your-nails-done-shouldn-t-make-you-sick-the-dangers-of-dipping-powder-and-mma  
**Date:** Jan 2, 2020  
**Read time:** 2 min  
**Author:** Beauy at your door

If you’re like many Canadian women, you probably enjoy treating yourself to nails for special occasions. Maybe you love them so much that you wear long nails all the time. Long nails are beautiful and sexy, right? Yes, but beware of the dangers of artificial nails. As the Canadian salon industry is not sufficiently regulated, some products and artificial nail techniques may be dangerous to your health.

Only three provinces have high standards for nail technician certification – Alberta, New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. This means that nail technicians in other Canadian provinces have mixed levels of training and education. Nails may look simple, but there are many important factors to be aware of to ensure safety. Nail techs need to learn about bacteriology and decontamination, first aid and artificial nail best practices. However, many young and inexperienced nail techs are using dangerous products and techniques.

Mom Susi Wermescher and her daughter Daniella Liebherr are co-owners of Beauty At Your Door, a mobile spa service in the west GTA. Originally from Germany, Susi and Daniella were trained to high international standards. After moving to Canada, Susi and Daniella have seen many questionable practices by other estheticians. Some former clients have had painful and unsightly experiences with other nail techs. Two of the biggest issues are dipping powder and MMA.

**Dipping powder**  
This is a drying agent which can cause serious fungal infections, which may result in nail or finger loss. Dip powder manicures, or powder manicures, are a popular beauty trend. A manicurist cleans your nails with an electric buffer, paints them with a clear adhesive, then dips each nail into a jar of finely grated pigmented powder. Usually, nails are dipped two or three times before being finished with a clear, quick-dry coat. This process makes the colour last longer. Technicians should be using an individual, clean jar of powder for each client. Any leftover powder must be disposed. If several people dip their nails in the same jar, germs can multiply and spread. The bottom line – avoid dipping powder.

**MMA**  
Methyl methacrylate (MMA) is a bonding agent used in artificial nail products. MMA has been banned in the nail industry for over 20 years but is still being used because it is cheap. This smelly, toxic chemical sets much faster than other products, and strongly adheres to your nails. MMA has been associated with adverse health effects. MMA-based acrylic nails will be tough, inflexible and difficult to remove. The long list of health risks includes irritation of eyes, nose, throat, respiratory tract, drowsiness, light-headedness, dizzy spells, trembling of hands, skin redness, itching, rash, swelling of fingers, infections, nail loss, infertility and cancer. Pretty scary stuff! Be sure that your nail tech does not use MMA. If you’re unsure, ask. Red flags: If the price is too good to be true, they use drills, your tech doesn’t speak English, and a strong smell.

## 18. Acrylic nails at your home
**URL:** https://www.beautyservicesatyourdoor.com/post/acrylic-nails-at-your-home  
**Date:** Aug 22, 2018 (Updated: Jan 2, 2020)  
**Read time:** 1 min  
**Author:** Beauy at your door

I get asked very often if I can do acrylic nails at clients homes. The answer to that is: NO

Why: Acrylics have as strong smell and that is something I don't want in any clients home. Acrylic nails are much stronger than Gel nails and I had to come up with a perfect solution for my clients. Polygel is the solution to the problem. This fairly new Gel is stronger than Gel yet softer than acrylics without the nasty smell. A lot of my clients switched to this extraordinary nail application and are very happy to wear long lasting beautiful nails for weeks.

Ask your local nail tech to only use safe products.
